Text:
This design is the inference of a simplified Fearless.
P is a program in the surface language.
X is a type variable, C is the 'name' of a literal, Par is a parameter and MName is the name of a method.
MName[Xs](Par1:T1 .. Parn:Tn):T 'declares' the X of Xs and Par1..Parn; C[Xs]:CTs{TMs} declares the X of Xs.
The inference of a method body fails when P.elaborate is undefined for that method.
The typing P; G |- CE : CT of the core language has no Notation in this design.

Grammar:
#this in Par
P ::= Top1 .. Topn
Top ::= C "[" Xs "]:" CTs "{" TMs "}"
TM ::= CSig SBody
CE ::= Par | CL | CE "." MName "[" CTs "](" CEs ")"
CL ::= C "[" Xs "]:" CTs "{" CMs "}"
CM ::= CSig CBody
CBody ::= ";" | "->" CE ";"
CSig ::= MName "[" Xs "](" Par1 ":" CT1 .. Parn ":" CTn "):" CT
CT ::= X | C "[" CTs "]"
E ::= Bare ":" T
Bare ::= Par | L | E "." MName Targs "(" Es ")"
Targs ::= "[" Ts "]" | ""
L ::= C "[" Xs "]:" Ts "{" Ms "}" | "{" Ms "}"
M ::= Sig Body
Body ::= ";" | "->" E ";"
Sig ::= MName "[" Xs "](" Par1 ":" T1 .. Parn ":" Tn "):" T
T ::= "?" | X | C "[" Ts "]"
SE ::= Par | SL | SE "." MName STargs "(" SEs ")"
STargs ::= "[" STs "]" | ""
SL ::= C "[" Xs "]:" CTs "{" SMs "}" | "{" SMs "}"
SM ::= SSig SBody
SBody ::= ";" | "->" SE ";"
SSig ::= MName "[" Xs "](" Par1 ":" ST1 .. Parn ":" STn "):" ST
ST ::= CT | "?"
G ::= Par1 ":" T1 .. Parn ":" Tn
Xs ::= X1 .. Xn
CTs ::= CT1 .. CTn
Ts ::= T1 .. Tn
STs ::= ST1 .. STn
CEs ::= CE1 .. CEn
Es ::= E1 .. En
SEs ::= SE1 .. SEn
TMs ::= TM1 .. TMn
CMs ::= CM1 .. CMn
Ms ::= M1 .. Mn
SMs ::= SM1 .. SMn

Well formedness:
in P: no two elements of P.SLs of form C[Xs]:CTs{SMs} have the same name
in P: there are no C1..Cn with n >= 1 and C(n+1) = C1 where, for each i in 1..n, Ci[_Xs]:_CTs C(i+1)[_CTs] _CTs{_SMs} is in P.SLs
in P: if C[_Xs]:_CTs{_SMs} is in P.SLs and is not one of the Tops of P, then no _C[_Xs]:_CTs C[_CTs] _CTs{_SMs} is in P.SLs
in P: if CSig and CSig' are in P.sigs(C[X1 .. Xk]) and CSig.MName = CSig'.MName, then CSig ~ CSig'
in Top: an SSig inside the SBody of an SM declares no X and no Par that the SSig of that SM declares, and no X that Top declares
in Top: in an SL of form C[Xs]:CTs{SMs} inside an SBody, every X of Xs is declared by Top or by an SSig with that SL inside its SBody
in Top: in an SL of form C[Xs]:CTs{SMs} inside an SBody, every X inside CTs{SMs} is in Xs or is declared by an SSig inside SMs
in SL: if SL is C[_Xs]:CT1 .. CTn{_SMs}, then every CTi is of form C[CTs]
in SMs: SMs is SM1 .. SMn with SM1.MName .. SMn.MName disjoint
in SSig: SSig is _MName[X1 .. Xk](Par1:_ST .. Parn:_ST):_ST with X1..Xk disjoint, Par1..Parn disjoint, and no Pari = #this
in G: G is Par1:_T .. Parn:_T with Par1..Parn disjoint

Notation: typeOf(_Bare:T) = T

Notation: ? ⊓ T = T
Notation: T ⊓ ? = T
Notation: X ⊓ X = X
Notation: C[Ts] ⊓ C[Ts'] = C[Ts⊓Ts']
Notation: T1 .. Tn ⊓ T'1 .. T'n = T1⊓T'1 .. Tn⊓T'n
Notation: meet() = ?
Notation: meet(T Ts) = T ⊓ meet(Ts)
Notation: Bare:T ⊓ T' = Bare:T'' where:
- T'' = T ⊓ T'

Notation: ?[Xs=Ts] = ?
Notation: X[X1 .. Xn=T1 .. Tn] = Ti where:
- X = Xi
Notation: X[Xs=Ts] = X where:
- X not in Xs
Notation: C[T1 .. Tn][Xs=Ts] = C[T1[Xs=Ts] .. Tn[Xs=Ts]]

Notation: refine(X; X; T) = T
Notation: refine(X; X'; T) = ? where:
- X != X'
Notation: refine(X; C[CT1 .. CTn]; C[T1 .. Tn]) = meet(refine(X;CT1;T1) .. refine(X;CTn;Tn))
Notation: refine(X; C[CT1 .. CTn]; T) = ? where:
- there are no T1..Tn with T = C[T1 .. Tn]
Notation: refines(X1 .. Xk; CT0 .. CTn; T0 .. Tn) = T'1 .. T'k where:
- for each h in 1..k: T'h = meet(refine(Xh;CT0;T0) .. refine(Xh;CTn;Tn))

Notation: close(Xs; ?) = ?
Notation: close(Xs; X) = ? where:
- X in Xs
Notation: close(Xs; X) = X where:
- X not in Xs
Notation: close(Xs; C[T1 .. Tn]) = C[close(Xs;T1) .. close(Xs;Tn)]

Notation: MName[Xs](Par1:T1 .. Parn:Tn):T0 ~ MName[Xs'](Par1:T1[Xs=Xs'] .. Parn:Tn[Xs=Xs']):T0[Xs=Xs'] where:
- Xs' has as many elements as Xs, and the elements of Xs' are disjoint
- each X of Xs' is in Xs or occurs in none of T0..Tn

Notation: MName[Xs'](Par1:T1[Xs=Ts] .. Parn:Tn[Xs=Ts]):T0[Xs=Ts] in P.sigs(C[Ts]) where:
- C[Xs]:_CTs{_SMs SSig _SBody _SMs} in P.SLs
- SSig ~ MName[Xs'](Par1:T1 .. Parn:Tn):T0
- Xs has as many elements as Ts
- no X of Xs' is in Xs or occurs in Ts
Notation: Sig in P.sigs(C[Ts]) where:
- C[Xs]:_CTs CT _CTs{_SMs} in P.SLs
- Sig in P.sigs(CT[Xs=Ts])

Notation: P.header(C[_Ts], MName) = X1 .. Xk; CSig where:
- X1..Xk are disjoint
- CSig in P.sigs(C[X1 .. Xk])
- CSig.MName = MName

Notation: inferenceOf(Par) = Par:?
Notation: inferenceOf(SE0.MName STargs(SE1 .. SEn)) = inferenceOf(SE0).MName STargs(inferenceOf(SE1) .. inferenceOf(SEn)):?
Notation: inferenceOf(C[Xs]:CTs{SM1 .. SMn}) = C[Xs]:CTs{inferenceOf(SM1) .. inferenceOf(SMn)}:?
Notation: inferenceOf({SM1 .. SMn}) = {inferenceOf(SM1) .. inferenceOf(SMn)}:?
Notation: inferenceOf(SSig;) = SSig;
Notation: inferenceOf(SSig->SE;) = SSig->inferenceOf(SE);

Notation: P.lower(Xs; Par:_CT) = Par
Notation: P.lower(Xs; E0.MName[CTs](E1 .. En):_CT) = P.lower(Xs;E0).MName[CTs](P.lower(Xs;E1) .. P.lower(Xs;En))
Notation: P.lower(Xs; C[Xs']:CTs{M1 .. Mn}:_CT) = C[Xs']:CTs{P.lower(Xs;M1) .. P.lower(Xs;Mn)}
Notation: P.lower(Xs; {M1 .. Mn}:C[CTs]) = C'[Xs]:C[CTs]{P.lower(Xs;M1) .. P.lower(Xs;Mn)} where:
- C' is the name of no literal of P and of no other literal that P.lower makes
Notation: P.lower(Xs; CSig;) = CSig;
Notation: P.lower(Xs; MName[Xs'](Par1:CT1 .. Parn:CTn):CT0->E;) = MName[Xs'](Par1:CT1 .. Parn:CTn):CT0->P.lower(Xs Xs';E);

Notation: P; G Par:T G' |- Par:T' ==> G Par:T'' G' |- Par:T'' where:
- T'' = T ⊓ T'
//(callNope)
Notation: P; G0 |- E0.MName Targs(E1 .. En):T ==> G(n+1) |- E'0.MName Targs(E'1 .. E'n):T where:
- P.header(typeOf(E0), MName) is undefined
- for each i in 0..n: P; Gi |- Ei ==>* G(i+1) |- E'i
//(callI)
Notation: P; G |- E0.MName(Es):T ==> G |- E0.MName[?1 .. ?k](Es):T where:
- P.header(typeOf(E0), MName) = _Xs; MName[X1 .. Xk](_):_
//(callR)
Notation: P; G0 |- E0.MName[Ts2](E1 .. En):T ==> \
  G(n+1) |- E'0⊓C[Ts3].MName[Ts4](E'1⊓CT1[Xs1 Xs2=Ts3 Ts4] .. E'n⊓CTn[Xs1 Xs2=Ts3 Ts4]):meet(T CT0[Xs1 Xs2=Ts3 Ts4]) where:
- P.header(typeOf(E0), MName) = Xs1; MName[Xs2](_Par:CT1 .. _Par:CTn):CT0
- for each i in 0..n: P; Gi |- Ei ==>* G(i+1) |- E'i
- typeOf(E'0) = C[Ts1]
- Ts3 Ts4 = Ts1 Ts2 ⊓ refines(Xs1 Xs2; CT0 CT1 .. CTn; T typeOf(E'1) .. typeOf(E'n))
- Ts3 has as many elements as Ts1
//(anon)
Notation: P; G1 |- {M1 .. Mn}:C[Ts1] ==> G(n+1) |- {M'1 .. M'n}:C[Ts(n+1)] where:
- for each i in 1..n: P; Gi; C[Tsi] |- Mi ==> G(i+1); C[Ts(i+1)] |- M'i
//(named)
Notation: P; G1 |- C[Xs]:Ts{M1 .. Mn}:T ==> G(n+1) |- C[Xs]:Ts{M'1 .. M'n}:T⊓C[Xs] where:
- for each i in 1..n: P; Gi; C[Xs] |- Mi ==> G(i+1); C[Xs] |- M'i
//(meth)
Notation: P; G; C[Ts] |- MName[Xs](Par1:T1 .. Parn:Tn):T0->E; ==> G'; close(Xs; C[Ts']) |- \
  MName[Xs](Par1:meet(T''1 CT1[Xs'=Ts']) .. Parn:meet(T''n CTn[Xs'=Ts'])):meet(T''0 CT0[Xs'=Ts'])->E'⊓CT0[Xs'=Ts']; where:
- P.header(C[Ts], MName) = Xs'; MName[Xs](_Par:CT1 .. _Par:CTn):CT0
- for each i in 0..n: T'i = meet(Ti CTi[Xs'=Ts])
- P; G Par1:T'1 .. Parn:T'n |- E⊓T'0 ==>* G' Par1:T''1 .. Parn:T''n |- E'
- T''0 = typeOf(E')
- Ts' = Ts ⊓ refines(Xs'; CT0 .. CTn; T''0 .. T''n)
//(methAbs)
Notation: P; G; C[Ts] |- MName[Xs](Par1:T1 .. Parn:Tn):T0; ==> G; C[Ts] |- \
  MName[Xs](Par1:meet(T1 CT1[Xs'=Ts]) .. Parn:meet(Tn CTn[Xs'=Ts])):meet(T0 CT0[Xs'=Ts]); where:
- P.header(C[Ts], MName) = Xs'; MName[Xs](_Par:CT1 .. _Par:CTn):CT0
//(methNew)
Notation: P; G; C[Ts] |- MName[Xs](Par1:CT1 .. Parn:CTn):CT0->E; ==> G'; C[Ts] |- MName[Xs](Par1:CT1 .. Parn:CTn):CT0->E'; where:
- P.header(C[Ts], MName) is undefined
- P; G Par1:CT1 .. Parn:CTn |- E⊓CT0 ==>* G' Par1:CT1 .. Parn:CTn |- E'
//(methNewAbs)
Notation: P; G; C[Ts] |- CSig; ==> G; C[Ts] |- CSig; where:
- P.header(C[Ts], CSig.MName) is undefined

Notation: P; G |- E ==>* G |- E where:
- there are no G', E' where P; G |- E ==> G' |- E' and G' |- E' is not G |- E
Notation: P; G |- E ==>* G'' |- E'' where:
- P; G |- E ==> G' |- E'
- G' |- E' is not G |- E
- P; G' |- E' ==>* G'' |- E''

Notation: P.elaborate(C[Xs]; MName[Xs'](Par1:CT1 .. Parn:CTn):CT0->SE;) = MName[Xs'](Par1:CT1 .. Parn:CTn):CT0->CE; where:
- G = #this:C[Xs] Par1:CT1 .. Parn:CTn
- P; G |- inferenceOf(SE)⊓CT0 ==>* _G |- E
- CE = P.lower(Xs Xs'; E)
- P; G |- CE : CT0
