A simplified language to study the inference.
The simplified language avoids two complications of Fearless:
reference capabilities, and the inference of method signatures (writing just .m->).

Core language:
e::= x | L | e.m[Ts](es)
L::= C[Xs]:Ts{Ms}
M::= m[Xs](x1:T1..xn:Tn):T; | m[Xs](x1:T1..xn:Tn):T->e;
T::= X | C[Ts]

Inference layer:
ie::= x:iT | iL:iT | ie.m[iTs](ies) | ie.m(ies)
iL::= C[Xs]:iTs{iMs} | {iMs}
iM::= m[Xs](x1:iT1..xn:iTn):iT; | m[Xs](x1:iT1..xn:iTn):iT->ie;
iT::= ? | X | C[iTs]

User surface language:
se::= x | sL | se.m[Ts](ses) | se.m(ses)
sL::= C[Xs]:Ts{sMs} | {sMs}
sM::= m[Xs](x1:T1..xn:Tn):T; | m[Xs](x1:T1..xn:Tn):T->se;
